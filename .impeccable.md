## Design Context

### Users

The product is for a local knowledge worker who records video meetings with Notion Meeting AI and wants the results in an Obsidian vault without manual copy/paste. The user works locally, reviews notes after meetings, and needs confidence that sync will not leak secrets or overwrite notes unexpectedly.

### Brand Personality

Calm, utilitarian, and precise. The interface should feel like a focused editing workbench, not a marketing dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a restrained light theme, dense but readable layout, and clear editor-first hierarchy. The app should prioritize note review, sync status, and save actions over decoration.

### Design Principles

- Make sync state and file destination explicit.
- Keep Markdown editing central and predictable.
- Avoid ornamental dashboards, decorative gradients, and generic card grids.
- Prefer accessibility and keyboard-friendly controls over visual flourish.
- Treat Notion credentials and Obsidian vault paths as local operational configuration.
